# Clera — Isotretinoin Treatment Monitoring Companion

A London-first B2B2C digital-health business case for a clinician-supervised companion that helps patients complete isotretinoin treatment safely.

## My contribution

I owned the financial model and commercial-viability analysis:

- built a three-year provider-based revenue and cost model;
- tested £12K, £24K and £36K annual subscription scenarios;
- modelled the COGS/fixed-cost split and a 78.1% base-case gross margin;
- forecast adoption from 3 to 15 providers and assessed cash runway;
- developed an illustrative £300K equity plus £75K grant funding plan.
- added break-even, CAC/LTV, payback, runway and price-sensitivity decision analysis.

## Enhanced decision outputs

| Metric | Illustrative base case |
|---|---:|
| Break-even providers at £24K subscription | 23 |
| FY3 planned providers | 15 |
| Provider gap to operating break-even | 8 |
| CAC payback | 11.5 months |
| Runway at £180K annual burn | 25 months |

The enhanced analysis shows why attractive unit economics do not automatically imply company-level break-even. The recommended response is staged hiring, standardised onboarding, tiered pricing and earlier fundraising triggers.

> All financial outputs are student-built forecasts for commercial-viability testing, not historical company results or investment projections.
